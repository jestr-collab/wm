El taller

A real shop, with real steel.

El Charro Taco Carts has been building vending equipment in Los Angeles since 2002. More than a thousand customers have bought carts from us for street routes, catering, and restaurant lines.

Everything is stainless: griddles, steamers, convex comals, al pastor trompos, salsa stations, and propane setups. Paint is part of the build, and so is a remodel if you already own a cart. If something's off when it's done, we make it right.

We also build for bigger kitchens — including custom cart work for Caesars Palace. Same shop, same welders.
