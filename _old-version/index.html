The shop

Cut, fitted, and welded in Los Angeles.

El Charro Taco Carts has built custom vending equipment since 2002. More than a thousand customers have ordered carts for street service, catering, and restaurant lines.

The work is stainless steel: griddles, steamers, convex comals, al pastor trompos, salsa stations, propane, and the frames that carry them. Paint is part of the build. So is a remodel if you already own the cart.
