PHOTOS ON THE EL CHARRO SITE

img/hero.jpg       Big photo at the top (the topping bar in the shop). Replace it with any wide shop photo.
img/carts/*.jpg    Product photos. Names match the list in js/site.js.

CHANGING PRODUCT NAMES OR DESCRIPTIONS
Open js/site.js. Near the top is the list of products. Change the name or text in quotes and save.

ADDING A PRODUCT OR A PHOTO
1. Put the photo in img/carts/ (example: green-cart-1.jpg)
2. Copy one product line in js/site.js and change the name, text and photo name.
   Several photos of one cart: list them all, like ["green-cart-1.jpg", "green-cart-2.jpg"].
